- Do not enter rooms or disturb anyone; observe from the corridor.
- Do not record names or faces. Do not photograph people.
- Photos are allowed only of door schedules, notices or booking lists, and only if no personal data is visible.
- Take one snapshot of every room every 15 minutes (5 snapshots: 0, 15, 30, 45, 60 min).
