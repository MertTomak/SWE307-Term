### 5.2.1 Problem statement

**Context.** On our campus, classrooms, laboratories and equipment (cameras, projectors, laptops, lab devices) are used outside scheduled lectures for study, project work and group meetings. The university library already runs an online booking system for its study rooms ([TBD: name of the system, from INT-02]). For department classrooms, laboratories and loanable equipment, the booking process is expected to be informal: requests by e-mail, in person, on paper lists or in messaging groups. [TBD: evidence — survey Q3, INT-01, document review DOC-01 to DOC-05]

**The problem.** Because these bookings are informal and spread over several channels:

1. **Users cannot see what is free.** There is no shared, up-to-date view of which room, lab or equipment item is available at a given time, so students spend time searching for a free place. [TBD: evidence — survey Q4, Q5; observation event type A]
2. **Booked resources stay unused.** A place can be reserved and then not used (a *no-show*), and nothing releases it for someone else. [TBD: evidence — survey Q6, Q7; observation snapshot at 15 min; INT-01 L4, N4]
3. **Staff handle every request by hand.** Lab and administrative staff receive, check, confirm and record each request manually, and equipment loans need a manual approval step. [TBD: evidence — INT-01 L1–L3, L6, N3; INT-02 K6]
4. **There is no usage data.** Occupancy and no-show rates are not recorded, so neither staff nor management can plan with data. [TBD: evidence — INT-01 L7; INT-02 K7]
5. **There is no consequence for repeated no-shows.** Even where an online system exists (the library), there appears to be no penalty for users who repeatedly book and do not come. [TBD: evidence — INT-02 K5]

**Problem statement (one sentence).** *The informal, multi-channel booking of department classrooms, laboratories and equipment leaves users unable to see what is free, lets booked resources stay unused without being released, makes staff process every request by hand, and produces no data on how the resources are used.*

**Who is affected.** Students and academic staff (time lost searching, unfair access), Resource Admins (lab and administrative staff: manual work), and university management (no data for planning space and equipment).

### 5.2.3 Scope

The scope follows the project card. The pilot uses the 13 resources in the seed data (5 rooms, 4 labs, 4 equipment items; see 5.11, C13) because no inventory of the university's bookable resources was available. [TBD: replace with the real inventory if INT-01 N1 gives it]

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Web application usable on desktop and on a phone browser (responsive). | Native mobile apps (iOS, Android). |
| Three resource types: `ROOM` (department classrooms and meeting rooms), `LAB` (laboratories), `EQUIPMENT` (loanable devices). | **Library study rooms**: the library already has its own booking system ([TBD: name]). CampusSlot does not replace or integrate with it. |
| Three roles: `STUDENT`, `ACADEMIC_STAFF`, `RESOURCE_ADMIN`; self-registration for students and academic staff (F1). | Login with the university's single sign-on, and any connection to the student information system. Admin accounts are created by the team (seed data). |
| Resource search, filters and availability calendar (F2). | Lecture timetabling and course scheduling. Rooms used for scheduled lectures are not managed by CampusSlot. |
| Reservation with automatic checks of R1–R5 (F3); cancellation until the start time (R6, F4). | Recurring reservations (e.g., every Monday for a term). |
| QR check-in at the door, with the 6-digit code as a fallback (R7, F5). | Physical access control: door locks, key handover, card readers. |
| Automatic no-show marking, release of the resource and 7-day penalty (R8, R9, F6). | Fines, payments or any other sanction outside the booking system. |
| Admin approval of `EQUIPMENT` reservations (R5, F7). | Tracking the physical return, damage or maintenance of equipment. |
| Admin panel: resource management, occupancy and no-show reports, penalties (F8). | Campus-wide analytics beyond the occupancy and no-show reports. |
| In-app notifications for approval, rejection, no-show and penalty (F4). | E-mail, SMS and push notifications. |
| User interface in English (NFR-19). | A Turkish user interface. |
| Pilot with fictional seed data only (5.10.2). | Use with real student data before a legal check by the university (KVKK; 5.8.5, 5.10.2). |

**Constraints.** Fixed stack (React + Vite + TypeScript, Node.js + Express + TypeScript, PostgreSQL + Prisma, JWT), fixed calendar (18 Nov – 6 Dec 2026, code freeze 1 Dec), a team of eight students, and no budget for paid services beyond what 5.11 lists.
