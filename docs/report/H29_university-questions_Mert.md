**Subject: CampusSlot (SWE307 term project) — four questions on hosting and KVKK**

Hello,

We are a student team building CampusSlot, a campus resource reservation and
QR check-in system, as our SWE307 term project. For the pilot and the course
demo we store only fictional seed accounts, so none of these questions block
our submission — but we would like to record the university's answer in our
report's legal feasibility section, and they would matter before any real
deployment.

1. **Hosting region.** If this system were deployed for real use, would the
   university require the application and database to be hosted inside
   Turkey, or would hosting the data with a foreign provider (e.g. Render,
   Neon) be acceptable under an appropriate-safeguard instrument such as the
   KVKK Board's standard contract?

2. **Data subject requests.** Under KVKK Art. 11, who would be the designated
   contact to receive and answer access, correction and deletion requests
   from students and staff within the 30-day limit — the IT department, a
   data protection officer, or another office?

3. **Privacy notice wording.** Does the university have standard wording it
   requires for a KVKK Art. 10 information notice shown at registration, or
   should we draft our own for review?

4. **Legal basis for processing.** For a system like this (reservation,
   check-in and no-show records tied to a university account), would the
   university rely on KVKK Art. 5(2)(f) legitimate interest, Art. 5(2)(c)
   performance of a contract/terms of use, or another basis?

Thank you — happy to share our full legal feasibility section (5.10.2) and
data model if useful.

Best regards,
Mert Tomak, on behalf of the CampusSlot team (SWE307)
