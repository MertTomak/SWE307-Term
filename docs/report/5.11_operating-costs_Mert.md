## 5.11 Value and Benefit Analysis: Operating Costs and Discount Rate (draft)

All prices were read from the vendors' official pricing pages on **23 Nov 2026** (access date). The final hosting provider will be chosen by Görkem; the services below are candidates used only to estimate cost. Limitations and recommendations are stated where a figure could not be verified from an official source.

### 5.11.1 Assumptions

| ID | Assumption |
|---|---|
| C1 | USD amounts are converted at the TCMB forex selling rate for USD/TRY of **50.4936** (bulletin 2026/219, dated 20 Nov 2026, the latest bulletin available on 23 Nov 2026). Source: https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/kurlar/today.xml (accessed 23 Nov 2026). The rate is held constant for 12 months. |
| C2 | Vendor prices are list prices in USD that exclude VAT and other taxes (stated on the Vercel pricing page). VAT is not added. |
| C3 | Each paid plan is billed monthly for 12 months with no discounts and no usage overage. |
| C4 | The hourly rate for maintenance and support is **178.73 TL/hour**, a **lower bound**. It is the employer's monthly cost of a minimum-wage worker in 2026 for sectors other than manufacturing (40,214.03 TL, gross wage 33,030.00 TL plus employer SGK share at 19.75% and employer unemployment premium at 2%) divided by 225 hours: 40,214.03 / 225 = 178.7290, rounded to 178.73. Source: https://csgb.gov.tr/poco-pages/asgari-ucret/ (accessed 23 Nov 2026). 225 hours = 45 weekly hours ÷ 6 days × 30 days. Law No. 4857, Art. 63 sets the general weekly maximum at 45 hours (https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/anasayfa/MevzuatFihristDetayIframe?MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatNo=4857&MevzuatTertip=5, accessed 23 Nov 2026). The text of the law does not contain the number 225; the 6-day and 30-day conversion is our own arithmetic. A software developer costs more than a minimum-wage worker, so real cost is higher. A sensitivity row at 250 TL/hour (an unsourced team figure) is given in 5.11.3. |
| C5 | Maintenance and support effort is 100 hours/year in Scenario A and 200 hours/year in Scenario B. These are team judgement. |
| C6 | "Campus scale" means one backend instance, one small database and one frontend project for the whole university. No load test has been done. |
| C7 | For the database in Scenario B, Neon's own "typical spend" example (US$15/month for intermittent load and 1 GB) is used as the estimate. Real cost depends on usage. |
| C8 | Scenario A is a pilot setup. Free-tier terms restrict production use. Vercel's Terms of Service state that the Hobby plan may be used only "for your personal or non-commercial use" and that Vercel may disable Hobby deployments without notice (https://vercel.com/legal/terms, last updated 19 Oct 2026, section "Hobby Plan", accessed 23 Nov 2026). Render states that Free instances should not be used for production applications (https://render.com/docs/free, accessed 23 Nov 2026). The Neon pricing page describes the Free plan as "no time limits" (https://neon.com/pricing); its terms of service were not read. Recommendation: the free-tier terms must be reviewed before production use, and Scenario B is the setup meant for production. |
| C9 | The .com domain renewal price is assumed equal to the registration price shown. |

### 5.11.4 Discount rate for NPV

**Proposed rate: 36% per year (nominal).**

Basis: the TCMB one-week repo (policy) rate is **36.00%**. It was set by the MPC decision of 22 Oct 2026 (No. 2026-45), effective 23 Oct 2026. The decision text states that the Committee decided to lower the one-week repo auction rate from 37% to 36% (https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/9813c150-5629-41ee-aab4-ed4e3ad2b740/DUY2026-45.pdf?MOD=AJPERES; the decision number comes from the file name, and the PDF text was only partly readable). The meeting date of 22 Oct 2026 is stated in the MPC meeting summary No. 2026-47 (https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Duyurular/Basin/2026/DUY2026-47). The effective date comes from the TCMB "1 Hafta Repo" table, which lists 36.00 from 23.10.2026 (previous values 37.00 from 23.01.2026 and 38.00 from 12.12.2025): https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Temel+Faaliyetler/Para+Politikasi/Merkez+Bankasi+Faiz+Oranlari/1+Hafta+Repo (accessed 23 Nov 2026).

Reasons:
- The cash flows of the project are in nominal TL, so a nominal TL rate is used. A rate that ignores TL inflation would overstate the value of future savings.
- The policy rate is the return an institution can earn on safe TL funds, so it is the minimum return the project should beat.
- It is an official, dated figure that can be checked.

Limitation: the policy rate has no project-risk premium.

**Next MPC meeting:** 10 Dec 2026, with the meeting summary on 17 Dec 2026 (TCMB calendar: https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Temel+Faaliyetler/Para+Politikasi/PPK/2026, accessed 23 Nov 2026). The meeting falls after the 6 Dec delivery date. The rate may change before the report is read, so the NPV should state that 36% is valid as of 23 Nov 2026.

**Sensitivity range (±5 percentage points):** 31%, 36%, 41%. The NPV should be reported at all three rates.
