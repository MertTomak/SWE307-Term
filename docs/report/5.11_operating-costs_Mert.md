### 5.11.4 Discount rate for NPV

**Proposed rate: 36% per year (nominal).**

Basis: the TCMB one-week repo (policy) rate is **36.00%**. It was set by the MPC decision of 22 Oct 2026 (No. 2026-45), effective 23 Oct 2026. The decision text states that the Committee decided to lower the one-week repo auction rate from 37% to 36% (https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/9813c150-5629-41ee-aab4-ed4e3ad2b740/DUY2026-45.pdf?MOD=AJPERES; the decision number comes from the file name, and the PDF text was only partly readable). The meeting date of 22 Oct 2026 is stated in the MPC meeting summary No. 2026-47 (https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Duyurular/Basin/2026/DUY2026-47). The effective date comes from the TCMB "1 Hafta Repo" table, which lists 36.00 from 23.10.2026 (previous values 37.00 from 23.01.2026 and 38.00 from 12.12.2025): https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Temel+Faaliyetler/Para+Politikasi/Merkez+Bankasi+Faiz+Oranlari/1+Hafta+Repo (accessed 23 Nov 2026).

Reasons:
- The cash flows of the project are in nominal TL, so a nominal TL rate is used. A rate that ignores TL inflation would overstate the value of future savings.
- The policy rate is the return an institution can earn on safe TL funds, so it is the minimum return the project should beat.
- It is an official, dated figure that can be checked.

Limitation: the policy rate has no project-risk premium.

**Next MPC meeting:** 10 Dec 2026, with the meeting summary on 17 Dec 2026 (TCMB calendar: https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Temel+Faaliyetler/Para+Politikasi/PPK/2026, accessed 23 Nov 2026). The meeting falls after the 6 Dec delivery date. The rate may change before the report is read, so the NPV should state that 36% is valid as of 23 Nov 2026.

**Sensitivity range (±5 percentage points):** 31%, 36%, 41%. The NPV should be reported at all three rates.

### 5.11.5 Development costs

The team is unpaid, so development is an **opportunity cost**, not a cash outflow (C17). Both the cost side and the benefit side are valued at the same rate, C4 = 178.73 TL/hour, so the comparison is internally consistent. C4 is a **lower bound** (the employer cost of a minimum-wage worker); a software developer costs more, so the real opportunity cost is higher. A sensitivity at 250 TL/hour is in 5.11.11.

**Effort (C17).** The plan in `5.5_planning_Mert.md` runs from 18 Nov to 6 Dec 2026, which is 19 calendar days, with 7 members.

| Step | Calculation | Result |

No paid design tool, no paid CI and no certificate cost is assumed (HTTPS is included by Vercel and Render).

At 178.73 TL/h and 225 h/person-month (C4), that is 14.38 × 225 = 3,235.5 h, and 3,235.5 × 178.73 = **578,280.91 TL**, about **8.1 times** the figure above. ### 5.11.8 Net Present Value (NPV)

**Formula.** NPV = Σ (net cash flow in year *t*) / (1 + r)^*t*, for *t* = 0 … 3, with r = 0.36 (5.11.4).

**Discount factors.**

| t | Calculation | Discount factor |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 / 1.36⁰ | 1.000000 |
| 1 | 1 / 1.36 | 0.735294 |
| 2 | 1 / 1.36² = 1 / 1.8496 | 0.540657 |
| 3 | 1 / 1.36³ = 1 / 2.515456 | 0.397542 |
| | Sum of t = 1…3 (annuity factor) | **1.673494** |

Factors are shown rounded to 6 decimals; the totals below are computed at full precision.

**Discounted cash flow.**

| t | Net cash flow (TL) | × factor | Discounted (TL) | Cumulative discounted (TL) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | −71,313.27 | 1.000000 | −71,313.27 | −71,313.27 |
| 1 | +54,383.40 | 0.735294 | +39,987.79 | −31,325.48 |
| 2 | +54,383.40 | 0.540657 | +29,402.79 | −1,922.69 |
| 3 | +54,383.40 | 0.397542 | +21,619.70 | **+19,697.01** |

**NPV = −71,313.27 + 54,383.40 × 1.673494 = −71,313.27 + 91,010.28 = +19,697.01 TL**

**NPV is positive but small.** It is 28% of the development cost and only 5.7% of the undiscounted three-year benefit. At a 36% discount rate, a three-year project has very little room: the annuity factor is 1.67, so each lira of annual benefit is worth only 1.67 lira in total. The result is therefore sensitive to the benefit parameters, and 5.11.11 shows by how much.

### 5.11.9 Return on Investment (ROI)

ROI can be stated undiscounted or discounted. Both are given, with the formula, because they answer different questions.

**Undiscounted (simple) ROI over 3 years.**

ROI = (Total benefits − Total costs) / Total costs × 100

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Total benefits | 116,174.50 × 3 | 348,523.50 TL |
| Total costs | 71,313.27 + (61,791.10 × 3) | 256,686.57 TL |
| Net | 348,523.50 − 256,686.57 | 91,836.93 TL |
| **ROI** | 91,836.93 / 256,686.57 × 100 | **35.78%** |

**Discounted ROI over 3 years** (same formula, present values at 36%).

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| PV of benefits | 116,174.50 × 1.673494 | 194,417.30 TL |
| PV of costs | 71,313.27 + (61,791.10 × 1.673494) | 174,720.29 TL |
| Net | 194,417.30 − 174,720.29 | 19,697.01 TL |
| **Discounted ROI** | 19,697.01 / 174,720.29 × 100 | **11.27%** |

The discounted ROI of 11.27% is the honest figure to quote, because the 36% discount rate is what the money could otherwise earn (5.11.4). A 35.78% simple ROI over three years ignores the time value of money entirely and would be misleading in a 36%-interest environment.

### 5.11.10 Payback period

**Simple payback.** The investment at t = 0 is recovered from a constant net annual benefit:

Payback = 71,313.27 / 54,383.40 = **1.31 years ≈ 15.7 months**

**Discounted payback.** From the cumulative column in 5.11.8, the cumulative discounted cash flow is still negative at the end of year 2 (−1,922.69 TL) and positive at the end of year 3 (+19,697.01 TL). Interpolating within year 3:

Discounted payback = 2 + (1,922.69 / 21,619.70) = 2 + 0.09 = **2.09 years**

The 0.78-year gap between the two figures is the cost of the 36% discount rate. The discounted payback of 2.09 years leaves less than one year of the three-year horizon as net gain, which is the same message as the small NPV.

### 5.11.13 Limitations, and what the survey must measure

1. **Five of the seven benefit parameters are unmeasured PM proposals** (C13 to C16). The base case rests mainly on C14 (3,900 bookings/year) and C15 (10 minutes per booking).
2. **C15 decides the answer.** Below roughly 9 minutes per booking the project does not pay for itself over three years at 36%.
